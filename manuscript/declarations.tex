\paragraph{Data availability statement.}
This study is a reanalysis of public spatial transcriptomics data; no new data were
generated, and every library below is named with the repository that serves it. The
four-patient CosMx basal-cell carcinoma export that carries the wound axis and the donor
split is at Zenodo, \texttt{doi:10.5281/zenodo.14330691}~\citep{Andreatta2024zenodo,Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint}. The CosMx
non-small-cell lung and human liver libraries are the NanoString NSCLC and human liver
FFPE releases, taken from the redistribution at Zenodo
\texttt{doi:10.5281/zenodo.15487520} and governed by the NanoString data licence
deposited in that record~\citep{He2022,Sun2025spatial}. That agreement is the Bruker
Spatial Biology Data License Agreement for Non-Commercial Use. It permits non-commercial
redistribution of the data and of derived reference matrices provided that a copy of the
licence travels with them, modifications are marked, NanoString is attributed with a link
to its website, and the same non-commercial terms are carried forward. Those terms are not
compatible with a CC-BY supplement, so the matrices derived from that release stay under
the deposited licence rather than the article terms. The CosMx colorectal sections are
at Zenodo \texttt{doi:10.5281/zenodo.15574384}~\citep{Crowell2025preprint}. The CosMx
pancreatic library is at NCBI GEO under accession
\texttt{GSE277782}~\citep{Pei2025}. The HNSCC openST library and the breast Xenium and
Xenium FLEX libraries are the public releases of their source
publications~\citep{Schott2024,Janesick2023}. The values plotted behind every panel, the
constants frozen before any sweep, the content digest of every locked input, and the
scripts that render every figure and table are provided as Supplementary Material; the
reference matrices are derived from the public libraries named above and are
identified in that bundle by digest rather than redistributed under their source
licences. The bundle will be assigned a versioned archival record when the editorial
process requires a permanent release. Requests for any input not covered above should go
to the corresponding author.

\paragraph{Ethics statement.}
Ethical review and informed consent were obtained in the primary studies that generated
the tissue and are reported there~\citep{Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint,He2022,Janesick2023,Schott2024,Crowell2025preprint,Pei2025}.
This work used only de-identified, already public data and did not recruit participants,
collect new specimens, or access identifiable information, so it required no additional
ethical approval.
