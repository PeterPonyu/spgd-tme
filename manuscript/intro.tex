Neighborhood-scale spatial assays record mixed observations rather than isolated cells \citep{Stahl2016,Moses2022,Rao2021}. Mixed-spot tumor-microenvironment (TME) composition is the readable quantity those assays produce, and per-spot cell-type proportions turn each mixed observation into a simplex for tissue interpretation \citep{Cable2022,Kleshchevnikov2022,Danaher2022,Miller2022,Newman2019,Elhanani2023}. Neighborhood assays instantiate this object here on CosMx basal-cell carcinoma, CosMx non-small-cell lung carcinoma, CosMx colorectal carcinoma, and CosMx hepatocellular carcinoma \citep{He2022,Andreatta2024zenodo,Crowell2025preprint,Sun2025spatial}, CosMx pancreatic carcinoma \citep{Pei2025}, HNSCC openST \citep{Schott2024}, and breast Xenium \citep{Janesick2023}. This paper estimates the per-spot simplex and adds an explicit rule for when the malignant coordinate is reportable.

Training-free and probabilistic composition estimators already recover spatial mixtures, including RCTD and cell2location \citep{Cable2022,Kleshchevnikov2022}, DestVI, CARD, and Tangram \citep{Lopez2022,Ma2022,Biancalani2021}, and stereoscope, SPOTlight, SpatialDWLS, and STdeconvolve \citep{Andersson2020,ElosuaBayes2021,Dong2021,Miller2022}. Independent benchmarks place RCTD and cell2location among leading composition estimators \citep{Li2022integrate,Sangaram2024}. The fixed baseline implementation SPGD is one such training-free estimator, specificity-weighted, self-gating, and platform-corrected, and its constants stay locked here \citep{Fu2026spgd}.

The standalone SPGD-TME contribution adds two locked operators to the released SPGD baseline \citep{Fu2026spgd} for reportable mixed-spot TME composition. The collinearity gate uses the frozen cutoff \(c^\star=0.80\): cosines at or above the cutoff return ABSTAIN and renormalize the remaining types, while cosines below the cutoff return KEEP and report a tumor fraction, a cosine-threshold refusal of the same kind used to leave a label unassigned when a reference match is not reportable \citep{Kiselev2018,deKanter2019}. Native calls at \(t=0\) return KEEP on CosMx BCC (cosine \(0.6037\)), independent CosMx NSCLC (cosine \(0.6482\)), CosMx colorectal carcinoma (cosine \(0.5058\)), and CosMx HCC (cosine \(0.6486\)) \citep{He2022,Andreatta2024zenodo,Crowell2025preprint,Sun2025spatial}, and return ABSTAIN on CosMx PDAC \(0.8616\), openST \(0.9727\), Xenium \(0.9802\), and the orthogonal Xenium FLEX reference \(0.9770\) \citep{Pei2025,Schott2024,Janesick2023}. CosMx PDAC abstains on the same platform that carries all four KEEP calls. The observed pattern is consistent with malignant--neighbor collinearity, while the library-level design does not establish platform independence. The second operator splits mixed spots from the reference by Patient\_ID, so a directed edge never trains on the patient whose spots it reads, matching the disjoint-reference construction used by mixed-spot readers \citep{Cable2022,Kleshchevnikov2022}.

Four-patient CosMx BCC is the mixed-spot object \citep{Andreatta2024zenodo,Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint}, and cell-level geography separates ulcerated-nodular Patients A and B from the wound time course on Patients C and D \citep{Yerly2025preprint,Dvorak1986}. Those fields become reportable products: a stromal versus tumor-rich ulcerated-nodular pair; a wound axis on which tumor fraction falls from \(0.8425\) to \(0.4665\) while fibroblast and MoMacDC rise \citep{Yerly2025preprint,Dvorak1986}; KEEP tumor coordinates written beside locked tumor truth; a collinearity dose that keeps CosMx below \(c^\star\) at \(t=0,0.1,0.2\); directed donor recovery whose tightest computed edge is B from A (RMSE \(0.0659\), spot-level PCC \(0.9528\)); and MoMacDC occupancy on the computed pairs. An evaluation board collects the \(t=0\) interval, the full-\(n\) confirmation, donor RMSE, and the timed pass, and mean truth fraction for each of eight shared types sits on a builder floor of fifty reference cells per type. The same frozen cosine then reports KEEP on independent CosMx NSCLC, colorectal carcinoma, and HCC \citep{He2022,Crowell2025preprint,Sun2025spatial}; directed Patient\_ID transfer on NSCLC recovers composition on 4237 spots (overall RMSE \(0.1066\), spot-level PCC \(0.8415\)). CosMx PDAC at cosine \(0.8616\) is the complementary ABSTAIN face of the same gate \citep{Pei2025}. Colorectal and hepatocellular KEEP calls instantiate that gate on additional CosMx carcinomas rather than a second wound time course.

Basal-cell carcinoma wound biology anchors the CosMx donor experiment through the classic wound-vs-tumor-stroma axis \citep{Dvorak1986} and cancer transcriptional hallmarks in the BCC cohort \citep{Gavish2023,Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint,Andreatta2024zenodo}. Patients A and B are ulcerated-nodular, and Patients C and D follow a wound time course. Because one CosMx slide can carry two people, the second operator's donor key is Patient\_ID, so a directed edge builds mixed spots and reference from disjoint patients.

Three claims follow \citep{Elhanani2023,Cable2022}. First, the two operators recover named tissue objects on four-patient CosMx BCC: an ulcerated-nodular contrast, an ordered wound axis, a reportable tumor column, and directed donor composition on three computed edges \citep{Andreatta2024zenodo,Yerly2025preprint}. Second, one frozen cosine supplies both faces of the gate across seven locked libraries and one orthogonal reference, and both faces occur on CosMx; the observed pattern is consistent with malignant-neighbor collinearity, while the library-level design does not establish platform independence \citep{Kiselev2018,deKanter2019,He2022,Crowell2025preprint,Pei2025}. Third, the reportability operation remains small relative to the fixed baseline fit: five warm 400-spot runs take \(22.565\pm2.861\) s, while 1,000 in-memory applications take \(0.455\) s.
