Neighborhood-scale spatial assays record mixed observations rather than isolated cells \cite{Stahl2016,Moses2022,Rao2021}. Mixed-spot tumor-microenvironment (TME) composition is the readable quantity those assays produce, and per-spot cell-type proportions turn each mixed observation into a simplex for tissue interpretation \cite{Cable2022,Kleshchevnikov2022,Danaher2022,Miller2022,Newman2019,Elhanani2023}. Neighborhood assays instantiate this object here on CosMx basal-cell carcinoma, CosMx non-small-cell lung carcinoma, CosMx colorectal carcinoma, and CosMx hepatocellular carcinoma \cite{He2022,Andreatta2024zenodo,Crowell2025preprint}, CosMx pancreatic carcinoma \cite{Pei2025}, HNSCC openST \cite{Schott2024}, and breast Xenium \cite{Janesick2023}. This paper estimates the per-spot simplex and adds an explicit rule for when the malignant coordinate is reportable.

Training-free and probabilistic composition estimators already recover spatial mixtures, including RCTD and cell2location \cite{Cable2022,Kleshchevnikov2022}, DestVI, CARD, and Tangram \cite{Lopez2022,Ma2022,Biancalani2021}, and stereoscope, SPOTlight, SpatialDWLS, and STdeconvolve \cite{Andersson2020,ElosuaBayes2021,Dong2021,Miller2022}. Independent benchmarks place RCTD and cell2location among leading composition estimators \cite{Li2022integrate,Sangaram2024}. The parent stack SPGD is one such training-free estimator, and its constants stay locked here.

Two locked operators extend SPGD for reportable mixed-spot TME composition. The collinearity gate uses the frozen cutoff \(c^\star=0.80\): cosines at or above the cutoff return ABSTAIN and renormalize the remaining types, while cosines below the cutoff return KEEP and report a tumor fraction, a cosine-threshold refusal of the same kind used to leave a label unassigned when a reference match is not reportable \cite{Kiselev2018,deKanter2019}. Native calls at \(t=0\) return KEEP on CosMx BCC (cosine \(0.603666\)), independent CosMx NSCLC (cosine \(0.648151\)), CosMx colorectal carcinoma (cosine \(0.505826\)), and CosMx HCC (cosine \(0.648597\)) \cite{He2022,Andreatta2024zenodo,Crowell2025preprint}, and return ABSTAIN on CosMx PDAC \(0.861599\), openST \(0.972678\), Xenium \(0.980196\), and the orthogonal Xenium FLEX reference \(0.987505\) \cite{Pei2025,Schott2024,Janesick2023}. One frozen cosine yields platform-dependent KEEP/ABSTAIN calls. The second operator splits mixed spots from the reference by Patient\_ID, so a directed edge never trains on the patient whose spots it reads, matching the disjoint-reference construction used by mixed-spot readers \cite{Cable2022,Kleshchevnikov2022}.

Four-patient CosMx BCC is the mixed-spot object \cite{Andreatta2024zenodo,Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint}, and cell-level geography separates ulcerated-nodular Patients A and B from the wound time course on Patients C and D \cite{Yerly2025preprint,Dvorak1986}. Those fields become reportable products: a stromal versus tumor-rich ulcerated-nodular pair; a wound axis on which tumor fraction falls from \(0.8425\) to \(0.4665\) while fibroblast and MoMacDC rise \cite{Yerly2025preprint,Dvorak1986}; KEEP tumor coordinates written beside locked tumor truth; a collinearity dose that keeps CosMx below \(c^\star\) at \(t=0,0.1,0.2\); directed donor recovery whose tightest computed edge is B from A (RMSE \(0.0659\), spot-level PCC \(0.9528\)); and MoMacDC occupancy on the computed pairs. An evaluation board collects the \(t=0\) interval, the full-\(n\) confirmation, donor RMSE, and the timed pass, and mean truth fraction for each of eight shared types sits on a builder floor of fifty reference cells per type. The same frozen cosine then reports KEEP on independent CosMx NSCLC, colorectal carcinoma, and HCC \cite{He2022,Crowell2025preprint}; directed Patient\_ID transfer on NSCLC recovers composition on 4237 spots (overall RMSE \(0.1066\), spot-level PCC \(0.8415\)). CosMx PDAC at cosine \(0.861599\) is the complementary ABSTAIN face of the same gate \cite{Pei2025}. Colorectal and hepatocellular KEEP calls instantiate that gate on additional CosMx carcinomas rather than a second wound time course.

Basal-cell carcinoma wound biology anchors the CosMx donor experiment through the classic wound-vs-tumor-stroma axis \cite{Dvorak1986} and cancer transcriptional hallmarks in the BCC cohort \cite{Gavish2023,Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint,Andreatta2024zenodo}. Patients A and B are ulcerated-nodular, and Patients C and D follow a wound time course. Because one CosMx slide can carry two people, the second operator's donor key is Patient\_ID, so a directed edge builds mixed spots and reference from disjoint patients.

The object is mixed-spot TME composition under the two operators \cite{Elhanani2023,Cable2022}. The CosMx carcinoma cohort contributes \(232{,}802\) cells from four patients, reduction to eight shared types leaves \(216{,}949\) typed cells, and expression-binned mixed spots give \(1194\), \(2020\), and \(3261\) spots on A from B, B from A, and D from C \cite{Andreatta2024zenodo}. The operators recover four named products: a reportable CosMx tumor column, wound-axis occupancy on Patients C and D, directed donor composition on the three computed edges, and independent KEEP calls on CosMx NSCLC, colorectal carcinoma, and HCC \cite{He2022,Crowell2025preprint,Yerly2025preprint}. The same frozen cosine supplies those KEEP/ABSTAIN calls across the locked libraries \cite{Kiselev2018,deKanter2019}.
