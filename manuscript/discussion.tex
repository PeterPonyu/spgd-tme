On four-patient CosMx basal-cell carcinoma the operators recover named tissue objects \cite{Andreatta2024zenodo,Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint}. Ulcerated-nodular Patient B is tumor-rich relative to the more stromal Patient A. The wound time course on Patients C and D is a drop in tumor fraction with a rise in fibroblast and MoMacDC, recovered on D-from-C mixed spots (Baseline tumor \(0.8425\) to Wound \(0.4665\); MoMacDC \(0.0070\) to \(0.1280\)) and in the same direction on typed Patient D cells \cite{Yerly2025preprint,Dvorak1986}.

The protocol is the computational contract: a KEEP/ABSTAIN gate at frozen \(c^\star=0.80\) and a Patient\_ID split that builds mixed spots and reference from disjoint patients \cite{Kiselev2018,deKanter2019,Cable2022}. Mixed-spot construction on four-patient CosMx BCC supplies the tissue object \cite{Stahl2016,Miller2022}, and cell-level geography makes the ulcerated-nodular and wound axes readable before any transfer \cite{Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint}. Spatial fields then name those axes: Patient A is more stromal and Patient B is tumor-rich, and tumor occupancy falls along the wound axis while fibroblast and MoMacDC rise \cite{Dvorak1986,Yerly2025preprint}. The protocol writes a CosMx tumor column beside locked tumor truth on 5686 full-\(n\) KEEP spots (median \(0.6739\)). One frozen cosine produces a platform-dependent KEEP window and remains KEEP on CosMx at \(t=0,0.1,0.2\) \cite{He2022,Kiselev2018}.

Donor transfer recovers mixed-spot truth across patients \cite{Cable2022,Kleshchevnikov2022}. Overall RMSE on the computed edges is \(0.0659\)--\(0.1349\), type-level Pearson correlation is \(0.7034\)--\(0.7708\), and spot-level Pearson correlation reaches \(0.9528\) on B from A. D from C carries the wound axis \cite{Yerly2025preprint}. The C-from-D row completes the four-edge donor matrix. MoMacDC occupancy is an axis on the computed pairs. The evaluation board collects the \(t=0\) interval, the full-\(n\) confirmation, donor RMSE, and the timed openST pass \cite{Schott2024}, and the fifty-cell reference floor sits under mean truth fraction for each of eight shared types.

The same frozen cosine reports KEEP on independent CosMx NSCLC (tumor versus fibroblast \(0.648151\); overall RMSE \(0.1066\), spot-level PCC \(0.8415\)), CosMx colorectal carcinoma (\(0.505826\)), and CosMx HCC (\(0.648597\)) \cite{He2022,Crowell2025preprint}. NSCLC donor transfer is a second Patient\_ID edge, not a second wound axis; colorectal and hepatocellular KEEP calls are gate recurrences on independent CosMx carcinomas. CosMx PDAC at \(0.861599\) is the ABSTAIN face of the same frozen cutoff \cite{Pei2025}. Native calls remain KEEP on CosMx BCC (cosine \(0.603666\)) and on the three independent KEEP carcinomas, and remain ABSTAIN on CosMx PDAC, HNSCC openST (\(0.972678\)), breast Xenium (\(0.980196\)), and the orthogonal Xenium FLEX library (\(0.987505\)) \cite{Schott2024,Janesick2023,Pei2025}. A tumor coordinate is therefore a KEEP/ABSTAIN decision, and the remaining types continue on the simplex in either case \cite{Kiselev2018,deKanter2019,Danaher2022}.

Related mixed-spot readers recover composition~\cite{Cable2022,Kleshchevnikov2022,Lopez2022,Ma2022,Biancalani2021,Danaher2022,Andersson2020,ElosuaBayes2021,Dong2021,Miller2022}. Independent maps place RCTD and cell2location among the leading composition estimators~\cite{Li2022integrate,Sangaram2024}. This companion keeps those parent constants locked and adds a reportability gate plus a Patient\_ID split, demonstrated on the CosMx wound and ulcerated-nodular axes and on independent CosMx KEEP carcinomas~\cite{Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint,Dvorak1986,He2022,Crowell2025preprint}.

Spatial TME maps read cellular composition in place~\cite{Elhanani2023,Rao2021,Moses2022}. The reportability gate is a cosine-threshold refusal: scmap leaves cells unassigned below a similarity cutoff, and CHETAH stops at unassigned or intermediate nodes when confidence is insufficient~\cite{Kiselev2018,deKanter2019}. Wound-axis occupancy remains a CosMx BCC object~\cite{Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint,Dvorak1986}. Independent CosMx KEEP calls on NSCLC, colorectal carcinoma, and HCC are recurrences of that gate on locked carcinomas, not a second wound axis~\cite{He2022,Crowell2025preprint}.
