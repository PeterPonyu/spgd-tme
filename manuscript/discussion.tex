The central contribution of SPGD--TME is a decision layer for mixed-spot composition. A composition vector is useful only when its coordinates are supported by separable reference programs. The KEEP/ABSTAIN operator makes that condition explicit: a malignant coordinate is written when the malignant--neighbor cosine is below the frozen threshold \(c^\star=0.80\), and it is withheld when the reference cannot distinguish the two programs. The other coordinates remain available after withholding the malignant coordinate. This partial report is different from discarding a spot, and it preserves information about the surrounding tissue while identifying where a tumor fraction should not be interpreted as a resolved measurement.

\paragraph{Reference separability and composition reporting.}
The CosMx basal-cell carcinoma cohort provides a useful test of this contract because the four-patient design contains both a tumor-rich ulcerated-nodular contrast and a wound-associated shift toward fibroblast and MoMacDC occupancy~\citep{Andreatta2024zenodo,Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint,Dvorak1986}. The frozen gate returns KEEP for the CosMx BCC malignant--neighbor pair (cosine \(0.6037\)), and the resulting tumor column agrees with locked truth over all 5686 paired spots (RMSE \(0.1516\), Pearson correlation \(0.9464\)). The spatial result is interpretable at the same time: tumor occupancy falls from \(0.8425\) at Baseline to \(0.4665\) at Wound, while fibroblast rises from \(0.0881\) to \(0.2107\) and MoMacDC from \(0.0070\) to \(0.1280\). Thus reportability does not replace biological interpretation; it identifies when that interpretation is supported by the reference geometry.

The dose-response experiments make the mechanism more specific. Interpolating a malignant signature toward its neighbor raises tumor error as the cosine approaches one. CosMx remains KEEP at \(t=0,0.1,0.2\) and changes to ABSTAIN at \(t=0.3\), whereas openST and Xenium begin above the cutoff (0.9727 and 0.9802) and remain withheld throughout the sweep. The 400-spot intervals and full-\(n\) estimates agree at \(t=0\), including the full-\(n\) CosMx RMSE of \(0.1516\) on 5686 spots. These results support a mechanistic interpretation of the gate as a separability rule rather than a fit-quality score. They do not imply that \(0.80\) is a universal optimum: it is a fixed operational threshold, and all eight historical calls remain unchanged between \(0.75\) and \(0.85\).

The cross-library pattern is consistent with this interpretation. Four CosMx libraries return KEEP and CosMx PDAC returns ABSTAIN at \(0.8616\), despite sharing the assay platform. The other ABSTAIN calls are openST HNSCC (0.9727), breast Xenium (0.9802), and Xenium FLEX (0.9770). A complete eligible-neighbor scan gives library maxima of \(0.847\) (NSCLC), \(0.791\) (CRC), \(0.854\) (HCC), and \(0.905\) (PDAC). Scored as a pair-level population, the complete scan holds 112 eligible malignant--neighbor pairs across the eight libraries and three platform families, with cosines from \(0.0707\) to \(0.9802\) (median \(0.6730\)); \(24.1\%\) reach \(0.80\) or higher (library-clustered bootstrap 95\% interval \(11.8\)--\(41.0\%\)). Because neighbor rows inside one library share a malignant column, the platform comparison is run with the library as the exchangeable unit: permuting the platform label over libraries by exact enumeration gives \(p=0.36\) for the between-family range in mean pair cosine and \(p=0.91\) for the between-family range in the fraction reaching the cutoff. The spread in mean pair cosine across the five CosMx libraries (\(0.312\)) is in fact larger than the spread between platform families (\(0.191\)). Accordingly, the data are consistent with collinearity as the immediate determinant of reportability, and provide no evidence that platform identity is the operative variable; with eight libraries in three families, however, this design is underpowered, so platform independence is not established as a general causal property. The historical designated pair and the complete scan answer different questions and are reported separately for that reason.

\paragraph{Donor-disjoint transfer and tissue structure.}
Patient\_ID disjointness addresses a separate source of optimism. A directed edge uses one patient's cells to create mixed spots and another patient's cells to build the reference, so no cell contributes to both sides of an evaluation edge. On the BCC cohort, the three computed edges give overall RMSE values from \(0.0659\) to \(0.1349\), type-level Pearson correlations from \(0.7034\) to \(0.7708\), and spot-level correlations up to \(0.9528\) for B from A. The malignant column is assessed separately (RMSE \(0.1031\)--\(0.1627\)), and pseudobulk Jensen--Shannon divergences remain \(0.0240\)--\(0.0432\). This three-part scoring prevents a well-recovered abundant type from hiding a poorly recovered malignant coordinate. The NSCLC transfer extends the same construction to a seventeen-type simplex (RMSE \(0.1066\), spot-level correlation \(0.8415\)), showing that the donor split is not restricted to the eight-type BCC reference.

The biological axes recovered by the transfer are visible before deconvolution. Patient B is tumor-rich relative to the more stromal Patient A, and the D-from-C edge orders the wound course in the same direction as the typed-cell fields. These observations matter for interpretation: the method recovers a composition contrast that is already present in the tissue, rather than creating a spatial pattern from an arbitrary reference. The builder floor of 50 cells per type supports all eight BCC types on the primary edges. In the bounded floor analysis, a floor of 25 retained 9--10 shared types, whereas floors of 50, 75, and 100 retained eight; estimates were identical from 50 through 100 on the evaluated edges because the retained support did not change.

\paragraph{Relation to existing estimators.}
Reference-based methods such as RCTD, cell2location, stereoscope, SPOTlight, SpatialDWLS, DestVI, CARD, Tangram, SpatialDecon, and STdeconvolve differ in their likelihoods, priors, or representation of within-type variation~\citep{Cable2022,Kleshchevnikov2022,Andersson2020,ElosuaBayes2021,Dong2021,Lopez2022,Ma2022,Biancalani2021,Danaher2022,Miller2022}. Their confidence fields answer method-specific questions about fit, posterior dispersion, or alignment. In the matched saved-output audit, malignant-coordinate RMSE was \(0.121\) for RCTD, \(0.154\) for SPGD, and \(0.249\) for cell2location on the common 400 spots; cell2location supplied a mean native interval width of \(0.0593\), whereas the saved RCTD output contained no matched per-spot interval field. These comparisons provide context rather than a universal ranking. The distinct contribution here is an explicit missing-coordinate state that can be applied around a composition estimate, including estimates from another underlying estimator. A lower RMSE or narrower interval does not by itself answer whether a malignant coordinate should be written.

\paragraph{Scope and limitations.}
The locked truth is a cell-count share, so each typed cell contributes one unit regardless of cell size, RNA content, or capture efficiency. This estimand is appropriate for the available cell-resolved construction but is not transcript mass. The saved truth does not contain a cell-to-spot transcript-total mapping, so an RNA-weighted sensitivity cannot be inferred from these data. Similarly, ABSTAIN renormalization is a conditional composition: the retained coordinates sum to one after the malignant coordinate is withheld. The conditional-neighbor audit preserved simplex closure (maximum absolute error \(1.1\times10^{-15}\)) but showed conditional biases of \(+0.0162\), \(+0.0214\), and \(-0.0004\) and conditional-neighbor RMSE of \(0.0853\), \(0.1682\), and \(0.1439\) on the openST, Xenium, and independent CosMx benchmark, respectively. An ABSTAIN report should therefore be read as composition conditional on the unreported malignant coordinate, not as an unbiased estimate of the neighbor's absolute occupancy.

The complete scan also shows why pair specification matters. The historical calls use biologically designated malignant--neighbor pairs, while the scan compares every eligible annotated non-malignant type and reports the maximum as a descriptive identifiability measure. Neighbor identity is partly confounded with library and platform in the current collection, and the number of complete rows differs between libraries. The resulting evidence supports a reportability contract and a useful audit of reference geometry, but it is not a platform-wide calibration study. Finally, raw provider-controlled inputs and derived reference materials remain subject to their data-use terms; the public analysis materials identify the exact locked outputs and rendering procedures without making a blanket redistribution claim for licensed inputs. Clinical outcome inference is outside the scope of this Methods study.

The computational cost of the added decision layer is small relative to composition fitting. On the repeated CosMx and openST benchmarks, the complete 400-spot analysis required \(22.565\pm2.861\) s over five warm CPU runs (CV \(12.68\%\)), while 1000 in-memory reportability applications required \(0.455\) s. Together with the materials ledger and donor-disjoint evaluation and the explicit withheld-coordinate state, these measurements define a practical Methods object and protocol for reporting where a mixed-spot tumor fraction is supported and where the surrounding composition should be interpreted conditionally.
