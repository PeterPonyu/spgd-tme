The central contribution of SPGD--TME is a decision layer for mixed-spot composition. A composition vector is useful only when its coordinates are supported by separable reference programs. The KEEP/ABSTAIN operator makes that condition explicit: a malignant coordinate is written when the malignant--neighbor cosine is below the frozen threshold \(c^\star=0.80\), and it is withheld when the reference cannot distinguish the two programs. The other coordinates remain available after withholding the malignant coordinate. This partial report is different from discarding a spot, and it preserves information about the surrounding tissue while identifying where a tumor fraction should not be interpreted as a resolved measurement.

The CosMx basal-cell carcinoma cohort provides a useful test of this contract because the four-patient design contains both a tumor-rich ulcerated-nodular contrast and a wound-associated shift toward fibroblast and MoMacDC occupancy~\citep{Andreatta2024zenodo,Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint,Dvorak1986}. The frozen gate returns KEEP for the CosMx BCC pair, and the resulting tumor column agrees with locked truth over all paired spots. Tumor occupancy falls from Baseline to Wound. Locked-truth fibroblast and MoMacDC occupancy both rise; the estimate recovers the MoMacDC rise monotonically and only part of the fibroblast rise. The spatial result stays interpretable on the coordinates that the estimate actually recovers. Thus reportability does not replace biological interpretation; it identifies when that interpretation is supported by the reference geometry.

The dose-response experiments make the mechanism more specific. These results support a mechanistic interpretation of the gate as a separability rule rather than a fit-quality score. They do not imply that \(0.80\) is a universal optimum: it is a fixed operational threshold, and all eight historical calls remain unchanged between \(0.75\) and \(0.85\).

The cross-library pattern is consistent with this interpretation. Four CosMx libraries return KEEP while CosMx PDAC returns ABSTAIN at \(0.8616\) on the same assay platform, and the remaining ABSTAIN calls are openST HNSCC (0.9727), breast Xenium (0.9802), and Xenium FLEX (0.9770). Read as a pair-level population rather than as eight library calls, the complete scan puts the spread in mean pair cosine across the five CosMx libraries (\(0.321\)) above the spread between platform families (\(0.191\)), and the permutation test run with the library as the exchangeable unit rejects on neither statistic. Because eight libraries cannot power that test, the variance decomposition carries the quantitative form of the claim: \(77.3\%\) of the pair-cosine variance sits between neighbor pairs inside one library against \(10.5\%\) between platform families. The largest observed variance component is therefore the identity of the two programs being compared rather than the assay that measured them. This is an association in the geometry of the reference, read off eight libraries with a permutation test the same eight libraries cannot power; it identifies where the variation sits and does not establish collinearity as the cause of a reportability call, nor platform independence as a general property. The platform term is imprecisely estimated and is not zero. The historical designated pair and the complete scan answer different questions and are reported separately for that reason.

Patient\_ID disjointness addresses a separate source of optimism. A directed edge uses one patient's cells to create mixed spots and another patient's cells to build the reference, so no cell contributes to both sides of an evaluation edge. On the BCC cohort the three computed edges report whole-simplex, malignant-column, and bulk-agreement scores side by side. This three-part scoring prevents a well-recovered abundant type from hiding a poorly recovered malignant coordinate. The NSCLC transfer extends the same construction to a seventeen-type simplex, showing that the donor split is not restricted to the eight-type BCC reference.

The biological axes recovered by the transfer are visible before deconvolution. Patient B is tumor-rich relative to the more stromal Patient A, and the D-from-C edge orders the wound course in the same direction as the typed-cell fields. These observations matter for interpretation: the method recovers a composition contrast that is already present in the tissue, rather than creating a spatial pattern from an arbitrary reference.

Reference-based methods such as RCTD, cell2location, stereoscope, SPOTlight, SpatialDWLS, DestVI, CARD, Tangram, SpatialDecon, and STdeconvolve differ in their likelihoods, priors, or representation of within-type variation~\citep{Cable2022,Kleshchevnikov2022,Andersson2020,ElosuaBayes2021,Dong2021,Lopez2022,Ma2022,Biancalani2021,Danaher2022,Miller2022}. Their confidence fields answer method-specific questions about fit, posterior dispersion, or alignment. In the matched saved-output audit no estimator wins on every field, and the saved outputs share no common per-spot interval field. These comparisons provide context rather than a universal ranking. The distinct contribution here is an explicit missing-coordinate state that can be applied around a composition estimate, including estimates from another underlying estimator. A lower RMSE or narrower interval does not by itself answer whether a malignant coordinate should be written.

The locked truth is a cell-count share, so each typed cell contributes one unit regardless of cell size, RNA content, or capture efficiency. This estimand is appropriate for the available cell-resolved construction but is not transcript mass. The saved truth does not contain a cell-to-spot transcript-total mapping, so an RNA-weighted sensitivity cannot be inferred from these data. Similarly, ABSTAIN renormalization is a conditional composition: the retained coordinates sum to one after the malignant coordinate is withheld. The conditional-neighbor audit preserved simplex closure (maximum absolute error \(1.1\times10^{-15}\)) but showed conditional biases of \(+0.0162\), \(+0.0214\), and \(-0.0004\) and conditional-neighbor RMSE of \(0.0853\), \(0.1682\), and \(0.1439\) on the openST, Xenium, and CosMx BCC benchmarks, respectively. An ABSTAIN report should therefore be read as composition conditional on the unreported malignant coordinate, not as an unbiased estimate of the neighbor's absolute occupancy.

The complete scan also shows why pair specification matters. The historical calls use biologically designated malignant--neighbor pairs, while the scan compares every eligible annotated non-malignant type and reports the maximum as a descriptive identifiability measure. The resulting evidence supports a reportability contract and a useful audit of reference geometry, but it is not a platform-wide calibration study. Finally, six of the eight libraries are obtainable from public repositories under no restrictive licence, while the CosMx non-small-cell lung and human liver libraries reach this work under a non-commercial data licence whose same-licence terms cannot travel with a CC-BY supplement; the derived matrices from that release are therefore identified by content digest rather than redistributed, and the restriction is on what this article may carry rather than on what a reader may obtain. Clinical outcome inference is outside the scope of this Methods study.

The computational cost of the added decision layer is small relative to composition fitting. Over five warm CPU runs of a 400-spot build the composition fit accounts for essentially the whole wall-clock cost, while the reportability decision applied to the CosMx benchmarks is under \(0.01\%\) of it. Together with the materials ledger and donor-disjoint evaluation and the explicit withheld-coordinate state, these measurements define a practical Methods object and protocol for reporting where a mixed-spot tumor fraction is supported and where the surrounding composition should be interpreted conditionally.
