Two locked operators turn a training-free composition estimator into one that recovers named tissue objects and states when its malignant coordinate is reportable, at a cost of one millisecond per pass. On four-patient CosMx basal-cell carcinoma the operators recover those objects \cite{Andreatta2024zenodo,Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint}. Ulcerated-nodular Patient B is tumor-rich relative to the more stromal Patient A. The wound time course on Patients C and D is a drop in tumor fraction with a rise in fibroblast and MoMacDC, recovered on D-from-C mixed spots (Baseline tumor \(0.8425\) to Wound \(0.4665\); MoMacDC \(0.0070\) to \(0.1280\)) and in the same direction on typed Patient D cells \cite{Yerly2025preprint,Dvorak1986}.

The protocol is the computational contract: a KEEP/ABSTAIN gate at frozen \(c^\star=0.80\) and a Patient\_ID split that builds mixed spots and reference from disjoint patients \cite{Kiselev2018,deKanter2019,Cable2022}. Mixed-spot construction on four-patient CosMx BCC supplies the tissue object \cite{Stahl2016,Miller2022}, and cell-level geography makes the ulcerated-nodular and wound axes readable before any transfer \cite{Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint}. Spatial fields then name those axes: Patient A is more stromal and Patient B is tumor-rich, and tumor occupancy falls along the wound axis while fibroblast and MoMacDC rise \cite{Dvorak1986,Yerly2025preprint}. The protocol writes a CosMx tumor column beside locked tumor truth on 5686 full-\(n\) KEEP spots (median \(0.6739\)). One frozen cosine holds a KEEP window that survives interpolation on CosMx at \(t=0,0.1,0.2\) and closes only when the malignant column is blended into its neighbor \cite{He2022,Kiselev2018}.

Donor transfer recovers mixed-spot truth across patients \cite{Cable2022,Kleshchevnikov2022}. Overall RMSE on the computed edges is \(0.0659\)--\(0.1349\), type-level Pearson correlation is \(0.7034\)--\(0.7708\), and spot-level Pearson correlation reaches \(0.9528\) on B from A. D from C carries the wound axis \cite{Yerly2025preprint}, and MoMacDC occupancy separates the computed pairs in the same direction. Each edge is scored three ways---whole simplex, malignant column alone, and pseudobulk agreement---so a tight overall RMSE cannot hide a loose tumor column. The evaluation board collects the \(t=0\) interval, the full-\(n\) confirmation, donor RMSE, and the timed openST pass \cite{Schott2024}, and the fifty-cell reference floor sits under mean truth fraction for each of eight shared types.

The same frozen cosine reports KEEP on independent CosMx NSCLC (tumor versus fibroblast \(0.648151\); overall RMSE \(0.1066\), spot-level PCC \(0.8415\)), CosMx colorectal carcinoma (\(0.505826\)), and CosMx HCC (\(0.648597\)) \cite{He2022,Crowell2025preprint,Sun2025spatial}. NSCLC adds a second Patient\_ID transfer edge on a seventeen-type simplex, and colorectal and hepatocellular KEEP calls are gate recurrences on independent CosMx carcinomas. CosMx PDAC at \(0.861599\) is the ABSTAIN face of the same frozen cutoff on that same assay \cite{Pei2025}, which is what makes the cutoff a statement about collinear type pairs rather than about platforms. Native calls remain KEEP on CosMx BCC (cosine \(0.603666\)) and on the three independent KEEP carcinomas, and remain ABSTAIN on CosMx PDAC, HNSCC openST (\(0.972678\)), breast Xenium (\(0.980196\)), and the orthogonal Xenium FLEX library (\(0.987505\)) \cite{Schott2024,Janesick2023,Pei2025}. A tumor coordinate is therefore a KEEP/ABSTAIN decision, and the remaining types continue on the simplex in either case \cite{Kiselev2018,deKanter2019,Danaher2022}.

Spatial profiling reads cellular composition in place, and the mixed-spot readers built for it differ in how they regularize that composition rather than in whether they report one~\cite{Elhanani2023,Rao2021,Moses2022}. Reference-based estimators fit the spot from a profile matrix: RCTD under a Poisson model with an explicit platform effect~\cite{Cable2022}, cell2location under a hierarchical Bayesian model~\cite{Kleshchevnikov2022}, stereoscope under a negative-binomial likelihood~\cite{Andersson2020}, SPOTlight by seeded NMF regression~\cite{ElosuaBayes2021}, and SpatialDWLS by dampened weighted least squares~\cite{Dong2021}. Others change what the fit is conditioned on: DestVI resolves within-type continua rather than a single profile per type~\cite{Lopez2022}, CARD borrows composition between neighboring spots through a spatial prior~\cite{Ma2022}, Tangram aligns cells to positions instead of solving the simplex~\cite{Biancalani2021}, SpatialDecon carries a platform's own background model into the regression~\cite{Danaher2022}, and STdeconvolve drops the reference for a topic model~\cite{Miller2022}. Independent benchmarks place RCTD and cell2location among the leading estimators on this task~\cite{Li2022integrate,Sangaram2024}. What none of them exposes is the condition under which the malignant coordinate is identifiable at all: each returns a full simplex whether or not the reference separates the malignant program from the type it is collinear with, so a tumor fraction written at malignant--neighbor cosine \(0.987505\) is indistinguishable in the output from one written at \(0.603666\).

The refusal added here is not new as an idea, only as a location. scmap leaves a cell unassigned below a similarity cutoff, and CHETAH stops at an unassigned or intermediate node when confidence is insufficient~\cite{Kiselev2018,deKanter2019}; both withhold a label for one cell. Moving that refusal onto the mixed-spot simplex is what lets a per-spot composition carry a malignant coordinate only where the reference can support one, and it is deliberately partial: the remaining types are still reported, so an ABSTAIN spot is a composition with one coordinate missing rather than a discarded spot. Because the cutoff is frozen from the parent stack~\cite{Fu2026spgd} rather than fitted per substrate, the eight KEEP and ABSTAIN calls above are out-of-sample applications of one rule, and CosMx PDAC abstaining on the platform that carries all four KEEP calls is what distinguishes that rule from a platform effect~\cite{Pei2025,He2022,Crowell2025preprint,Sun2025spatial}.

Three boundaries delimit the claim: the wound axis is a CosMx BCC object on Patients C and D, so the independent NSCLC, colorectal, and hepatocellular results are gate and transfer recurrences rather than a second wound time course~\cite{Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint,Dvorak1986,He2022,Crowell2025preprint,Sun2025spatial}; the C-from-D row is recorded rather than recomputed here and is what closes the four-edge donor matrix; and the cutoff is frozen from the parent stack, so this work reports where that rule lands rather than an optimum. Clinical outcome analysis lies outside this Methods object.
