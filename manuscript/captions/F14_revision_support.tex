Truth-stratified error concentration and block-bootstrap uncertainty for the malignant coordinate. The analysis reports bias, absolute error, RMSE, and Pearson correlation within four equal-count malignant-truth strata for CosMx BCC at full \(n\), openST, Xenium, and the same CosMx BCC library on its 400-spot benchmark. Only the full-\(n\) CosMx BCC row uses tissue coordinates for its \(2\times2\) blocks; the three 400-spot rows block by row index modulo 4. No estimator or cutoff was refit. (A)~Block-bootstrap malignant-coordinate RMSE for the four substrates, with points showing the estimate and vertical bars showing the 95\% interval. (B)~Bias (estimate minus truth) within the four truth strata; rows are substrates and columns are increasing malignant-truth quartiles. (C)~Mean estimated malignant fraction against mean locked truth within each stratum. Binning is by locked truth, not by predicted value, so the panel is not a reliability diagram in the usual calibration sense. (D)~Mean absolute error by truth stratum, showing where numerical error concentrates even when a reportability decision is unchanged. These panels describe the accuracy profile that accompanies the KEEP/ABSTAIN state; they do not show that the gate's error risk is calibrated, and they do not turn a withheld coordinate into an observed tumor fraction.
