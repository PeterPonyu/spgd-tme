Truth-stratified calibration and spatial-block uncertainty for the malignant coordinate. The analysis reports bias, absolute error, RMSE, and Pearson correlation within four equal-count malignant-truth strata for CosMx BCC at full \(n\), openST, Xenium, and the same CosMx BCC library on its
400-spot benchmark, with a 2 by 2 spatial or deterministic block bootstrap; no estimator or cutoff was refit. (A)~Block-bootstrap malignant-coordinate RMSE for the four substrates, with points showing the estimate and vertical bars showing the 95\% interval. (B)~Bias (estimate minus truth) within the four truth strata; rows are substrates and columns are increasing malignant-truth quartiles, so the panel distinguishes calibration at low, intermediate, and high occupancy. (C)~Mean estimated malignant fraction against mean locked truth within each stratum; the diagonal is the calibration reference and the four coloured trajectories identify the rows, two of which are the same CosMx BCC library at two sample sizes. (D)~Mean absolute error by truth stratum, showing where numerical error concentrates even when a reportability decision is unchanged. These panels quantify the accuracy profile that accompanies the KEEP/ABSTAIN state; they do not turn a withheld coordinate into an observed tumor fraction.
