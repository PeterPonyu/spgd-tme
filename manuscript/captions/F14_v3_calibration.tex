Truth-stratified error concentration and block-bootstrap uncertainty for the malignant coordinate. The analysis reports bias, absolute error, RMSE, and Pearson correlation within four equal-count malignant-truth strata for CosMx BCC at full \(n\), openST, Xenium, and the same CosMx BCC library on its 400-spot benchmark. Only the full-\(n\) CosMx BCC row uses tissue coordinates for its \(2\times2\) blocks; the three 400-spot rows block by row index modulo 4. Binning is by locked truth, not by predicted value. These results describe where the existing coordinate is accurate and where mixed occupancy concentrates error; they do not refit the estimator, select a new cutoff, or show that the gate's error risk is calibrated.
