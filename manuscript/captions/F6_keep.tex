The protocol reports CosMx tumor coordinates and places them beside locked tumor truth. (A)~Patient C cells in packed 0.51 mm fields of view, coloured by cancer membership on \([0,1]\) (1 = Cancer.cells). (B)~Locked tumor truth on packed Patient C fields. Point colour is the per-spot tumor truth fraction on \([0,1]\) (cell-count share of Cancer.cells). (C)~Estimated tumor fraction on packed Patient C fields. Point colour is the protocol tumor coordinate on \([0,1]\). On the full-\(n\) CosMx KEEP map at \(t=0\) (\(n=5686\)), estimated tumor fraction has median \(0.6739\) (quartiles \(0.0510\)--\(0.9195\); mean \(0.5428\)). (D)~One Patient C field of view coloured by cancer membership on \([0,1]\). (E)~Estimated tumor fraction against locked tumor truth on the same full-\(n\) CosMx KEEP map, one point per spot (\(n=5686\)); the dashed line is identity. Tumor RMSE is \(0.1516\) and the Pearson correlation between the two columns is \(0.9464\). (F)~Cumulative distribution of locked tumor truth and of the protocol estimate over those 5686 spots, with the median level marked. The two run together up to about half occupancy and separate in the upper tail: truth saturates at \(1\) on \(27.3\%\) of spots, and \(7.0\%\) of estimates reach \(0.99\) or more.
