\paragraph{The four-patient CosMx mixed-spot object.}
Four-patient CosMx basal-cell carcinoma is the mixed-spot TME object. The export contains 232{,}802 cells from four patients~\citep{Andreatta2024zenodo}, and restriction to the eight shared types leaves 216{,}949 typed cells. Directed pairs A from B, B from A, and D from C yield 1194, 2020, and 3261 spots on mixed spots of 42.6--45.7\,\(\mu\)m pooling 17--21 cells each (Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}A, F), and the eight shared types carry mass on every pair (Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}B). On this object the frozen rule returns KEEP, because the CosMx malignant--neighbor cosine is \(0.6037\), below the cutoff \(c^\star=0.80\), while the three sweep substrates read against the same rule sit above it and abstain: openST \(0.9727\), Xenium \(0.9802\), and Xenium FLEX \(0.9770\) (Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}C). The four independent CosMx carcinomas the same cutoff is later asked to call are collected in Fig.~\ref{fig:keepcancers}A. The C-from-D metrics (overall RMSE \(0.1149\), type-level PCC \(0.7708\), spot-level PCC \(0.8773\)) are recorded in Table~\ref{tab:donor}.

\paragraph{Robustness of reportability and quantitative accuracy.}
The eight historical designated-pair calls are unchanged when the frozen threshold is evaluated from \(0.75\) through \(0.85\). A complete scan over represented eligible non-malignant columns provides a complementary sensitivity analysis: the maximum cosine is \(0.847\) (NSCLC), \(0.791\) (CRC), \(0.854\) (HCC), and \(0.905\) (PDAC). These values are descriptive properties of the reference geometry; the operating calls continue to use the biologically designated pair, while the complete scan is reported as an identifiability stress test. The stored export holds 46 scanned rows, of which 44 carry an eligible non-malignant label (8 colorectal, 17 NSCLC, 14 hepatocellular, 5 PDAC; the excluded rows are the unassigned \texttt{NotDet} and \texttt{Undetermined} columns). A library-clustered bootstrap over those 44 rows gives a mean cosine of \(0.697\) (95\% interval \(0.604\)--\(0.781\)) and \(34.1\%\) of rows at or above \(0.80\) (95\% interval \(8.0\)--\(68.4\%\)). The wide interval reflects dependence among rows from the same library and supports a graded interpretation of the cross-library result.

The withheld-coordinate audit quantifies the cost of reporting the remaining simplex conditionally. After excluding spots with zero retained truth mass, the conditional malignant-neighbor RMSE is \(0.0853\) for openST, \(0.1682\) for Xenium, and \(0.1439\) for independent CosMx benchmark; corresponding conditional biases are \(+0.0162\), \(+0.0214\), and \(-0.0004\). Zero-mass exclusions were \(66/400\), \(3/400\), and \(109/400\), respectively. When malignant and designated-neighbor mass are merged before scoring, RMSE is \(0.0707\), \(0.1655\), and \(0.1545\). Thus renormalization preserves closure but does not recover an absolute neighbor occupancy when the malignant coordinate is withheld; the reported vector is explicitly conditional.

Reference representation thinning was tested with 21 fresh fits (three seeded fits at 75\% and 50\% retained genes, plus the unthinned reference) across openST, Xenium benchmark, and independent CosMx benchmark. The historical state remained ABSTAIN for openST and Xenium and KEEP for CosMx in every fit. At 50\% retention, malignant RMSE changed from \(0.0762\) to \(0.0846\) (openST), from \(0.1682\) to \(0.1699\) (Xenium), and from \(0.1220\) to \(0.1423\) (CosMx). This separates stability of the reportability state from stability of the quantitative coordinate: the gate is robust to moderate representation thinning, whereas aggressive gene loss can reduce numerical accuracy.

Truth-stratified evaluation further localizes this error. With four equal-count truth strata and a block bootstrap, malignant RMSE was \(0.1516\) for CosMx BCC (95\% interval \(0.1337\)--\(0.1678\)), \(0.0757\) for openST (\(0.0720\)--\(0.0792\)), \(0.1558\) for Xenium (\(0.1454\)--\(0.1653\)), and \(0.1542\) for independent CosMx benchmark (\(0.1398\)--\(0.1684\)) (Fig.~\ref{fig:revision-support}). In CosMx BCC, the largest bias and RMSE occur in the middle and high truth strata, whereas the lowest-occupancy stratum is close to calibrated. This pattern motivates treating reportability as an identifiability statement that accompanies, rather than replaces, quantitative error reporting (Fig.~\ref{fig:revision-audit}).

The source-count floor remains a bounded sensitivity parameter. Floors of 50, 75, and 100 retain the same eight BCC types and identical estimates on the evaluated edges; a floor of 25 retains 9--10 shared types and changes malignant RMSE by \(0.004\), \(0.001\), and \(0.016\) on A-from-B, B-from-A, and D-from-C, respectively. On the matched 400 spots used for diagnostic comparison, malignant-coordinate RMSE is \(0.154\) for SPGD, \(0.249\) for cell2location, and \(0.121\) for RCTD. cell2location native interval width has mean \(0.0593\), while the saved RCTD output contains no matched per-spot interval field. These method-specific uncertainty fields are therefore reported as context and are not treated as interchangeable with the withheld-coordinate state.

\paragraph{Full-sample and independent-method consistency.}
The complete scan and the fresh comparator extend the sensitivity analysis beyond the four CosMx libraries shown above. Across all eight libraries, the designated-KEEP cosines range from \(0.5058\) to \(0.6486\), while designated-ABSTAIN cosines begin at \(0.8616\); the resulting empty interval contains \(c^\star=0.80\) and leaves every historical call unchanged from 0.75 to 0.85. A library-clustered bootstrap over the eight libraries gives an eligible-pair exceedance fraction of \(0.239\) (95\% interval \(0.119\)--\(0.400\)) on 109 eligible pairs; the full scan holds 112 malignant--neighbor columns, of which three (\texttt{NotDet}, \texttt{Undetermined}, and one unlabelled column) are non-annotated placeholders excluded from the eligible set. This eight-library scan is a separate full-input audit from the 44-row four-library table above, which uses the stored CosMx reference export and is retained to make denominator differences explicit. Because the eight-library scan spans three platform families, it also supports the platform comparison directly: permuting the platform label across libraries by exact enumeration, with the library as the exchangeable unit, gives \(p=0.37\) for the between-family range in mean pair cosine and \(p=0.91\) for the between-family range in exceedance fraction, while the spread across the five CosMx libraries (\(0.321\)) exceeds the spread between families (\(0.191\)). Because a permutation test on eight libraries has little power, we also decompose the pair-cosine variance directly, which does not depend on rejecting a null. Nested sums of squares over the 109 eligible pairs place \(10.5\%\) of the variance between platform families, \(12.2\%\) between libraries within a family, and \(77.3\%\) between neighbor pairs inside the same library. Collinearity is therefore predominantly a property of which two programs are being compared rather than of the assay that measured them, although the platform term is not zero and the three components are estimated from eight libraries. At full sample size, the P/S gate-input comparison reproduces the openST discrepancy (P \(=0.9727\), ABSTAIN; S \(=0.6895\), KEEP), while Xenium, Xenium FLEX, and CosMx BCC retain identical P and S decisions. This confirms that the distinction between the pre-gate profile and the fit signature is not caused by the bounded 400-spot probe.

On a fresh matched 800-spot CosMx BCC subset, SPGD outperforms a Tangram cluster refit on overall RMSE (\(0.108\) versus \(0.206\)), malignant RMSE (\(0.157\) versus \(0.491\)), type-level PCC (\(0.605\) versus \(0.330\)), spot-level PCC (\(0.844\) versus \(0.551\)), and JSD (\(0.094\) versus \(0.254\)). These values provide a direct external comparator for the composition estimate; they do not turn method-specific confidence fields into a common uncertainty scale. Finally, if the complete maximum were used as the operating rule on CosMx BCC, the malignant coordinate would be withheld and the retained non-malignant simplex would have RMSE \(0.214\), compared with \(0.106\) for the historical full-simplex score (Fig.~\ref{fig:fulln-comparator}). The contrast demonstrates why the designated pair is retained as the primary operating estimand and why the complete scan is valuable as a sensitivity audit rather than an automatic replacement.
