\paragraph{The four-patient CosMx mixed-spot object.}
Four-patient CosMx basal-cell carcinoma is the mixed-spot TME object. The export contains 232{,}802 cells from four patients~\cite{Andreatta2024zenodo}, and restriction to the eight shared types leaves 216{,}949 typed cells. Directed pairs A from B, B from A, and D from C yield 1194, 2020, and 3261 spots on mixed spots of 42.6--45.7\,\(\mu\)m pooling 17--21 cells each (Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}A, F), and the eight shared types carry mass on every pair (Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}B). On this object the frozen rule returns KEEP, because the CosMx malignant--neighbor cosine is \(0.603666\), below the cutoff \(c^\star=0.80\), while the three sweep substrates read against the same rule sit above it and abstain: openST \(0.972678\), Xenium \(0.980196\), and Xenium FLEX \(0.987505\) (Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}C). The four independent CosMx carcinomas the same cutoff is later asked to call are collected in Fig.~\ref{fig:keepcancers}A. Each pair is a distribution and not a mean: per-spot tumor truth has median \(0.2500\) on A from B, \(0.9661\) on B from A, and \(0.8824\) on D from C (Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}D). Occupancy is uneven across the simplex, each type holding between \(4.5\%\) and \(90.9\%\) of a pair's spots (Fig.~\ref{fig:hero}E), which is the sparsity every type-averaged score below is computed over. The C-from-D metrics (overall RMSE \(0.1149\), type-level PCC \(0.7708\), spot-level PCC \(0.8773\)) are recorded in Table~\ref{tab:donor}.

\paragraph{The gate tracks malignant--neighbor collinearity.}
Collinearity dose--response interpolates each malignant signature toward its neighbor (Fig.~\ref{fig:dose}A--C). At \(t=0\), the 400-spot ungated tumor RMSEs were \(0.0757\) (95\% bootstrap CI \(0.0643\)--\(0.0874\); \(B=1000\)), \(0.1558\) (\(0.1380\)--\(0.1724\)), and \(0.1542\) (\(0.1364\)--\(0.1729\)) for openST, Xenium, and CosMx. Full-\(n\) confirmation gave \(0.0903\) (\(n=6971\)), \(0.1613\) (\(n=2864\)), and \(0.1516\) (\(n=5686\)), so the 400-spot interval is not a small-sample artifact; both the interval and the full-\(n\) point sit at \(t=0\) on the same axis as the sweep. Across the sweep, openST RMSE increased from \(0.0757\) to \(0.3357\) and CosMx from \(0.1542\) to \(0.6862\). Read in the cutoff's own coordinate, that rise is monotone in the malignant--neighbor cosine and the refusal is a threshold on it (Fig.~\ref{fig:dose}D--F): CosMx travels from \(0.603666\) to \(1.0000\) and stays KEEP at \(t=0,0.1,0.2\), writing ABSTAIN from \(t=0.3\), while openST and Xenium start at \(0.972678\) and \(0.980196\) and therefore refuse across the whole sweep.

\paragraph{Directed donor transfer recovers composition across patients.}
Directed donor transfer recovers composition across patients (Fig.~\ref{fig:donor}A; Table~\ref{tab:donor}). \paragraph{Evaluation board and operator cost.}
The evaluation board collects the \(t=0\) interval (Fig.~\ref{fig:eval}A), the full-\(n\) confirmation (Fig.~\ref{fig:eval}B), donor RMSE (Fig.~\ref{fig:eval}C), and the timed openST pass (Fig.~\ref{fig:eval}D; Table~\ref{tab:timing}), beside the margin each native call holds against the cutoff (Fig.~\ref{fig:eval}E) and the pseudobulk agreement of each computed edge (Fig.~\ref{fig:eval}F). CosMx keeps \(0.1963\) of headroom below \(c^\star\) while openST, Xenium, and Xenium FLEX sit \(0.1727\)--\(0.1875\) above it, so no call is near the cutoff. The reportability gate costs \(0.001\) s of the \(60.252\) s pass, and the platform self-gate costs \(47.961\) s of it, so refusability is essentially free relative to the fit it guards. At whole-run scale the same conclusion holds: full-\(n\) \(t=0\) passes take \(267.696\) s on 6971 openST spots, \(234.554\) s on 5686 CosMx spots, and \(24.158\) s on 2864 Xenium spots, all on CPU.

\paragraph{Every reported type clears the reference floor.}
Every reported type clears the builder floor of fifty reference cells (Fig.~\ref{fig:floor}A), so the eight-type simplex is supported rather than extrapolated. Occupancy on that supported simplex is nonetheless concentrated, and the concentration is a matter of presence rather than of size: the malignant column is nonzero on \(90.9\%\) of B-from-A and \(85.3\%\) of D-from-C spots, whereas Pericyte is absent from \(95.5\%\) and \(87.2\%\) of the same spots (Fig.~\ref{fig:floor}B). Type-level PCC of \(0.7034\)--\(0.7708\) is therefore an average over one dense column and several near-empty ones. The wound axis sits on the same floor (Fig.~\ref{fig:floor}C), and packed A-from-B, B-from-A, and D-from-C fields render the same tumor-truth fractions as spatial occupancy (Fig.~\ref{fig:floor}D--F).

\paragraph{The same frozen cosine on independent CosMx carcinomas.}
The same frozen cosine reports KEEP on independent CosMx carcinomas (Fig.~\ref{fig:keepcancers}A). CosMx BCC, CosMx NSCLC, CosMx CRC, and CosMx HCC sit below \(c^\star=0.80\); CosMx PDAC, openST, Xenium, and Xenium FLEX sit above it, so the gate splits four CosMx KEEP calls from one CosMx ABSTAIN call on the same assay. Behind each reported number the malignant program was scored against every annotated type in its library (Fig.~\ref{fig:keepcancers}B), and that neighborhood is what separates the two faces: two of seventeen NSCLC neighbors reach the cutoff and none of the eight colorectal ones do, eight of fifteen do on hepatocellular carcinoma, and on PDAC all six do (Fig.~\ref{fig:keepcancers}F). PDAC therefore abstains because its malignant program is collinear with its entire neighborhood, not because of the assay it was measured on. Directed Patient\_ID transfer carries the NSCLC edge (4237 spots; overall RMSE \(0.1066\), spot-level PCC \(0.8415\)) beside the three BCC edges (Fig.~\ref{fig:keepcancers}C--E). That edge resolves a seventeen-type simplex rather than the eight BCC types, and its type-level PCC of \(0.5986\) is averaged over those seventeen columns.
