This Methods paper shows how to estimate which cell types occupy a mixed patch of tumor tissue, and how to withhold the tumor fraction when that number is not separable. The calculator is the existing training-free SPGD stack. What is new is a locked collinearity rule: if the tumor profile is too similar to a neighboring profile, the protocol writes ABSTAIN and continues on the remaining types. The rule is fixed before looking at the results. Tests use public head-and-neck, breast, and skin tumor spatial datasets, including a four-patient CosMx basal-cell carcinoma set split by person. The same skin set also supplies a wound-axis occupancy contrast on mixed spots.
