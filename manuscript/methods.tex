SPGD-TME is a standalone Methods implementation for reporting mixed-spot tumour composition. It combines a fixed composition-estimation core with two locked operators: a KEEP/ABSTAIN reportability gate and a patient-identity donor split (Fig.~\ref{fig:protocol}A--F). Related mixed-spot readers recover composition, including RCTD, cell2location, DestVI, and CARD~\citep{Cable2022,Kleshchevnikov2022,Lopez2022,Ma2022}. The scientific contribution is the explicit decision contract that states when a malignant coordinate is supported by separable reference programs and when it must be withheld. The accompanying public repository provides the frozen constants, reference tables, analysis scripts, renderers, and verification tests needed to reproduce the operator-inclusive results. The fixed composition core is cited for provenance and is held constant throughout all analyses; the cutoff, donor split, abstention, complete-neighbor sensitivity analysis, and new robustness experiments are evaluated here as one reproducible protocol.

\paragraph{Signatures and specificity weights.}
A TME reference supplies a gene-by-type matrix \(P\), column-normalized to a probability profile. The specificity of gene \(g\) across \(K\) types is one minus the normalized entropy of that gene's type profile \(q_g\), as given by Equation~\eqref{eq:specificity}:
\begin{equation}
w_g=1-\frac{H(q_g)}{\log K},\qquad H(q_g)=-\sum_{k=1}^{K} q_{gk}\log(q_{gk}+\varepsilon),\qquad \varepsilon=10^{-12}.
\label{eq:specificity}
\end{equation}
Marker genes receive large \(w_g\) under \eqref{eq:specificity}; genes shared across neighboring types receive small \(w_g\). The weighted signature and the weighted spot matrix are \(P\odot w\) and \(Y\odot w\). The numerical floor \(\varepsilon=10^{-12}\) is a fixed stability constant, not a fitted or swept hyperparameter: it makes the zero-probability terms in \(q\log q\) finite and is applied identically in every library. Reporting it is therefore part of the decision contract, because a reader can reproduce the entropy weights and the cutoff-adjacent cosine values without inferring a hidden numerical convention.

\paragraph{Platform self-gate and Poisson close.}
A per-gene platform factor \(\hat\gamma\) is fit by a bulk-ratio update on the weighted Poisson objective. The factor is applied only to the extent it generalizes across a held-out half of the spots. Let \(g\in[0,1]\) be that held-out improvement. In the fixed baseline implementation, spots are permuted with a fixed seed and split into halves A and B; relative held-out Poisson-deviance reductions are computed for A\(\to\)B and B\(\to\)A, averaged, and clipped to \([0,1]\). Thus \(g\) measures the transferable fraction of the platform correction supported by held-out fit. A value near zero means that a platform adjustment which improves one half does not transfer reliably to the other half; a larger value permits more of that adjustment into the working signature because the same direction improves held-out Poisson fit. This cross-half check makes the platform correction self-limiting: it cannot be granted full weight merely because it improves the spots used to estimate it. The operation uses no outcome labels or per-library threshold tuning, and is therefore part of the fixed baseline stage rather than a second training procedure. The working signature is given by Equation~\eqref{eq:working-signature}:
\begin{equation}
S=e^{g\hat\gamma}\,(P\odot w).
\label{eq:working-signature}
\end{equation}
Nonnegative loadings \(W^\star\) are obtained by multiplicative Poisson updates against \(S\) of \eqref{eq:working-signature}, then projected onto the simplex, as given by Equation~\eqref{eq:simplex}:
\begin{equation}
\hat\pi=\mathrm{simplex}(W^\star),\qquad \hat\pi_{s,k}\ge 0,\quad \sum_{k=1}^{K}\hat\pi_{s,k}=1.
\label{eq:simplex}
\end{equation}
Index \(s\) is a mixed spot and \(k\) is a type. The matrix \(\hat\pi\) of \eqref{eq:simplex} is the composition estimate. Cell-level fields in Fig.~\ref{fig:cohort}A--D are not \(\hat\pi\).

\paragraph{Mixed-spot construction and predictive sizing.}
CosMx cells are split by Patient\_ID~\citep{Andreatta2024zenodo,Yerly2025preprint}. Cells from the spot donor are aggregated from recorded coordinates into square spatial bins. Bins with fewer than five cells are dropped. The mixed-spot count vector is the gene-wise sum of member cells, and the mixed-spot coordinate is the mean of those cell coordinates. Display maps convert the native pixel frame to micrometres without exchanging axes. Let \(n_{s,k}\) be the number of typed cells of type \(k\) in spot \(s\). Locked mixed-spot truth is the cell-count-share estimand of Equation~\eqref{eq:truth-frac}:
\begin{equation}
\pi_{s,k}=\frac{n_{s,k}}{\sum_{k'=1}^{K}n_{s,k'}},\qquad \pi_{s,k}\ge 0,\quad \sum_{k=1}^{K}\pi_{s,k}=1.
\label{eq:truth-frac}
\end{equation}
On four-patient CosMx BCC, \(K=8\) with types Cancer.cells, Endothelial, Fibroblast, Melanocyte, MoMacDC, Normal.Kerat, Pericyte, and PlasmaCell. On the independent NSCLC edge, the same construction supplies \(\pi_{s,k}\) and \(\hat\pi_{s,k}\) on seventeen types. Approximate spot diameters are 45.7\,\(\mu\)m (A from B), 45.1\,\(\mu\)m (B from A), and 42.6\,\(\mu\)m (D from C), with median cell counts 17, 21, and 19. The reference for a directed edge is built from the other patient's cells, with a builder floor of fifty reference cells per type, a pre-agreed operating floor set to sit comfortably clear of the data rather than as an optimum: the smallest retained reference type in this cohort holds 137 cells, so the floor binds nowhere.

\paragraph{Occupancy and colour bar.}
Spot occupancy of type \(k\) is \(\pi_{s,k}\) of \eqref{eq:truth-frac}. Mean occupancy on an edge of \(n\) spots is given by Equation~\eqref{eq:mean-occupancy}:
\begin{equation}
\bar\pi_{k}=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{s=1}^{n}\pi_{s,k}.
\label{eq:mean-occupancy}
\end{equation}
Figure~\ref{fig:myeloid}A is the \(\bar\pi_{\mathrm{MoMacDC}}\) of \eqref{eq:mean-occupancy} together with the per-spot \(\pi_{s,\mathrm{MoMacDC}}\) it averages. A typed cell \(\ell\) carries the indicator of Equation~\eqref{eq:cell-indicator}:
\begin{equation}
I_{\ell,k}=\mathbf{1}\{\mathrm{type}(\ell)=k\}\in\{0,1\}.
\label{eq:cell-indicator}
\end{equation}
The typed-cell fraction is the cell-level share \(p_{k}^{\mathrm{cell}}=\sum_{\ell}I_{\ell,k}/\sum_{\ell}1\) built from \eqref{eq:cell-indicator}, not mixed-spot \(\pi_{s,k}\). Figure~\ref{fig:cohort}A--D maps \(\mathrm{type}(\ell)\) on packed fields, colouring each cell by whichever \(k\) has \(I_{\ell,k}=1\); panel E reports \(p_{k}^{\mathrm{cell}}\) by patient and condition; panel F reports the median number of cells pooled into a mixed spot. Figures encode \(\pi_{s,k}\) or \(I_{\ell,k}\) on a colour bar scaled to \([0,1]\). The scale is type occupancy, not gene expression. Panels that colour cells by their annotated type instead use one fixed eight-colour key, the Okabe--Ito colourblind-safe palette, held constant across every figure so a type keeps its colour throughout. Every spatial panel also carries a black rule with its length printed beneath it, in micrometres or millimetres on the CosMx pixel-to-micrometre conversion; axes are never stretched independently, so that rule is valid in both directions.

\paragraph{Reportability gate.}
The reportability gate is evaluated on the pre-gate reference profiles \(P\), after the same library normalization used to construct the reference, and before the platform-corrected working signature is used for the Poisson fit. Thus \(p_{\mathrm{mal}}\) and \(p_{\mathrm{nbr}}\) denote the locked malignant and neighbor columns of \(P\); \(S\) is retained for composition fitting and is not substituted into the gate. The reason is that the gate asks a question about the reference, not about the target library: whether the two programs are separable in the matrix used to define them. The platform factor \(\hat\gamma\) that turns \(P\) into \(S\) is fit on the spots being deconvolved, so a gate evaluated on \(S\) would let a per-gene rescaling estimated from the target data decide whether that same target's malignant coordinate is reportable, and the same locked reference would then be separable on one library and not on another for reasons external to the programs. The effect is not hypothetical: at full \(n\) the platform correction lowers the designated cosine on every audited library --- openST \(0.9727\) to \(0.6895\), Xenium \(0.9802\) to \(0.9447\), Xenium FLEX \(0.9770\) to \(0.9632\), CosMx BCC \(0.6037\) to \(0.5609\) --- and on openST it moves the call across the cutoff, so a gate on \(S\) would report a malignant coordinate that the reference cannot separate. Evaluating the gate before the correction keeps the decision a fixed property of the locked reference, which is what allows \(c^\star\) to be frozen once and audited across libraries.
Native cosine is the uninterpolated column cosine of Equation~\eqref{eq:native-cosine}:
\begin{equation}
\cos(p_{\mathrm{mal}},p_{\mathrm{nbr}})=\frac{p_{\mathrm{mal}}^{\top}p_{\mathrm{nbr}}}{\lVert p_{\mathrm{mal}}\rVert_2\,\lVert p_{\mathrm{nbr}}\rVert_2}.
\label{eq:native-cosine}
\end{equation}
The frozen threshold applied to \eqref{eq:native-cosine} is \(c^\star=0.80\), giving the gate of Equation~\eqref{eq:keep-gate}:
\begin{equation}
\cos(p_{\mathrm{mal}},p_{\mathrm{nbr}})\ge c^\star \;\Rightarrow\; \text{ABSTAIN},\qquad
\cos(p_{\mathrm{mal}},p_{\mathrm{nbr}})< c^\star \;\Rightarrow\; \text{KEEP}.
\label{eq:keep-gate}
\end{equation}
The value \(0.80\) was fixed as a round pre-sweep operating value carried over from the parent stack; it is not an optimum from a ROC analysis, a theoretical derivation, or a sweep over these libraries. On the KEEP branch of \eqref{eq:keep-gate} a tumor fraction \(\hat\pi_{s,\mathrm{mal}}\) is written. On ABSTAIN the malignant coordinate is held as missing and the remaining types are renormalized, as given by Equation~\eqref{eq:abstain}:
\begin{equation}
\hat\pi_{s,\mathrm{mal}}\leftarrow\mathrm{missing},\qquad
\hat\pi_{s,k}\leftarrow\frac{\hat\pi_{s,k}}{\sum_{k'\neq\mathrm{mal}}\hat\pi_{s,k'}}\quad(k\neq\mathrm{mal}).
\label{eq:abstain}
\end{equation}
The renormalization in \eqref{eq:abstain} leaves gated tumor RMSE on ABSTAIN missing, not zero. Because withholding a malignant coordinate can inflate a collinear neighbor, the remaining coordinates are interpreted as a conditional composition after malignant withholding; this potential bias is quantified in the revision sensitivity analysis rather than hidden by scoring the missing coordinate as zero. This is an intentional output state: an ABSTAIN spot is retained for the non-malignant composition question, while the unsupported malignant coordinate remains missing. The reported simplex is consequently conditional on withholding and must not be read as an unbiased estimate of the original full composition. Native calls at interpolation fraction \(t=0\) are CosMx BCC cosine \(0.6037\) (KEEP), CosMx NSCLC cosine \(0.6482\) (KEEP), CosMx colorectal cosine \(0.5058\) (KEEP), CosMx HCC cosine \(0.6486\) (KEEP), CosMx PDAC cosine \(0.8616\) (ABSTAIN), then openST cosine \(0.9727\), Xenium cosine \(0.9802\), and Xenium FLEX cosine \(0.9770\) (ABSTAIN).

\paragraph{Designated neighbor and complete-scan audit.}
The historical operating call uses a designated malignant--neighbor pair, and the procedure that designated it was not uniform across the eight libraries. For openST, Xenium, and CosMx BCC the pair identity is the entry in the locked constants file that ships with this article, and Xenium FLEX reuses the Xenium pair identity against an orthogonal reference rather than carrying a lock entry of its own; no analysis-time selection was applied to any of the four; the Xenium neighbor in that file had itself been taken as the highest-cosine member of a five-element candidate list, so its winner is a second tumor compartment rather than a stromal type and the procedure that produced it selects toward ABSTAIN. For the four independent CosMx carcinomas the neighbor was instead chosen at analysis time by walking a stromal-preference ordering and taking the first candidate that returned KEEP, falling back to the first candidate when every candidate abstained; that procedure reads the decision it is selecting for and is therefore outcome-dependent. In the hepatocellular library the ordering was additionally edited to place hepatic stellate cells ahead of the default immune entries after the macrophage and endothelial columns were seen to sit at or above the cutoff. The bias that procedure introduces runs toward KEEP, so it reaches exactly the three calls it returned as KEEP --- NSCLC, colorectal, and hepatocellular. It does not reach CosMx PDAC: every one of that library's six columns sits at or above the cutoff, the lowest at \(0.8036\), so the walk found no KEEP to select and the ABSTAIN it returned survives a procedure that was searching for the opposite answer. It does not reach the CosMx BCC application cohort either, whose pair was not chosen by this walk. We report the designated pair as a historical operating estimand on that basis rather than as a pre-specified rule, retain it as the primary estimand so that no reported call is silently replaced by a result-dependent maximum, and give the complete eligible scan beside it as a declared sensitivity analysis. The locked selection algorithm in the supplementary package governs future use; it does not describe how these eight calls were made. Both locks travel with the article, and the designated pairs and the cutoff were fixed before the complete eligible scan reported below was built; we do not assert a chronology earlier than that, because the constants file predates this article's version history and the package cannot evidence it. For the sensitivity analysis, the eligible set is defined independently of the decision, as given by Equation~\eqref{eq:eligible-neighbors}:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal E_L=\{k\in\mathcal T_L: k\neq k_{\mathrm{mal}},\; n_{L,k}\ge n_{\min},\; k\text{ is represented in the locked reference for }L\},
\label{eq:eligible-neighbors}
\end{equation}
where \(\mathcal T_L\) is the annotated type set for library \(L\), \(n_{L,k}\) is the available typed-cell count, and \(n_{\min}\) is the reference-support floor. The analysis reports both the historical designated value and the descriptive maximum \(\max_{k\in\mathcal E_L}\cos(p_{\mathrm{mal}},p_k)\). The maximum is a prespecified sensitivity estimand over all represented eligible non-malignant columns; it is reported alongside, rather than substituted for, the historical operating pair.

\paragraph{Interpolation dose.}
The collinearity sweep replaces the malignant signature by the interpolation of Equation~\eqref{eq:interpolate}:
\begin{equation}
s(t)=(1-t)\,s_{\mathrm{mal}}+t\,s_{\mathrm{nbr}},\qquad t\in\{0,0.1,\ldots,1.0\}.
\label{eq:interpolate}
\end{equation}
At each \(t\) of \eqref{eq:interpolate}, \eqref{eq:native-cosine} is evaluated on \(s(t)\) and \(s_{\mathrm{nbr}}\). Ungated tumor RMSE is recorded at every \(t\) on a 400-spot subset, a fixed compute budget that keeps the eleven-point sweep over three substrates (33 fits) comparable at one spot count. The KEEP/ABSTAIN overlay leaves gated tumor RMSE missing on refused \(t\). A spot bootstrap with \(B=1000\) supplies a percentile 95\% interval on the \(t=0\) tumor fractions. Full-\(n\) confirmation repeats \(t=0\) on 6971 openST, 2864 Xenium, and 5686 CosMx spots.

\paragraph{Donor split.}
Directed transfer uses Patient\_ID as the donor key, giving the disjointness of Equation~\eqref{eq:donor-split}:
\begin{equation}
\{\text{spot patient}\}\cap\{\text{reference patient}\}=\emptyset.
\label{eq:donor-split}
\end{equation}
Pairs satisfying \eqref{eq:donor-split} are condition-matched: A with B (Ulcerated nodular) and C with D (wound time course). This analysis computes A from B, B from A, and D from C. The C-from-D composition is the recorded four-edge close. The wound axis is read on Yerly BCC Patients C and D.

\paragraph{Evaluation matrix.}
Figure~\ref{fig:donor}A reports Overall RMSE, Tumor RMSE, Type PCC, and Spot PCC on each directed edge. With locked truth \(\pi_{s,k}\) and estimate \(\hat\pi_{s,k}\) on \(n\) spots and \(K\) types, the metrics of Equations~\eqref{eq:overall-rmse}--\eqref{eq:spot-pcc} are:
\begin{align}
\mathrm{RMSE}
&= \sqrt{\frac{1}{nK}\sum_{s=1}^{n}\sum_{k=1}^{K}(\hat\pi_{s,k}-\pi_{s,k})^{2}},
\label{eq:overall-rmse}\\
\mathrm{RMSE}_{\mathrm{mal}}
&= \sqrt{\frac{1}{n}\sum_{s=1}^{n}(\hat\pi_{s,\mathrm{mal}}-\pi_{s,\mathrm{mal}})^{2}},
\label{eq:tumor-rmse}\\
\mathrm{PCC}_{\mathrm{type}}
&= \frac{1}{|K_+|}\sum_{k\in K_+}\mathrm{corr}(\pi_{\cdot,k},\hat\pi_{\cdot,k}),
\label{eq:type-pcc}\\
\mathrm{PCC}_{\mathrm{spot}}
&= \frac{1}{|S_+|}\sum_{s\in S_+}\mathrm{corr}(\pi_{s,\cdot},\hat\pi_{s,\cdot}).
\label{eq:spot-pcc}
\end{align}
The correlation in \eqref{eq:type-pcc} and \eqref{eq:spot-pcc} is Pearson, as given by Equation~\eqref{eq:pearson}:
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{corr}(a,b)=\frac{\sum_{i}(a_i-\bar a)(b_i-\bar b)}{\sqrt{\sum_{i}(a_i-\bar a)^{2}}\,\sqrt{\sum_{i}(b_i-\bar b)^{2}}}.
\label{eq:pearson}
\end{equation}
Equation~\eqref{eq:overall-rmse} averages squared error over the full \(n\times K\) matrix, including every type, while \eqref{eq:tumor-rmse} evaluates the malignant column alone. \(K_+\) is the set of types with positive standard deviation in both truth and estimate, so Type PCC averages over all such types and includes the tumor column when that column varies. \(S_+\) is the set of spots with positive standard deviation across types. A computed edge also records pseudobulk Jensen--Shannon divergence between the mixed-spot bulk and the reference bulk. Figure~\ref{fig:donor}B--C carry the per-spot error and the composition each edge is scored against; Fig.~\ref{fig:myeloid}A plots the occupancy of \eqref{eq:mean-occupancy}.

\paragraph{Independent CosMx instantiations.}
The same frozen gate and the same Patient\_ID split \eqref{eq:donor-split} are then read on independent CosMx carcinomas. NSCLC uses pooled tumor versus fibroblast (cosine \(0.6482\)) and a directed Patient\_ID edge of 4237 spots on 17 types (overall RMSE \(0.1066\), type-level PCC \(0.5986\), spot-level PCC \(0.8415\), malignant-column RMSE \(0.3202\)). Those NSCLC donor metrics are \(\mathrm{RMSE}\), \(\mathrm{PCC}_{\mathrm{type}}\), \(\mathrm{PCC}_{\mathrm{spot}}\), and the malignant-column RMSE on the seventeen-type simplex. The malignant column is the loosest in this study: a KEEP call states that the reference can separate the two programs, not that the resulting coordinate is accurate, and the three-part scoring is reported here precisely so a well-recovered abundant type cannot stand in for it. CosMx CRC and CosMx HCC return KEEP at native cosines \(0.5058\) and \(0.6486\). CosMx PDAC returns ABSTAIN at cosine \(0.8616\).

\paragraph{Timing.}
Timing is reported from one openST 400-spot build repeated five times after a warm-up pass, decomposed into stages that do not overlap so that the stages sum to the build (Table~\ref{tab:timing}). The build takes \(16.103\pm2.891\) s over runs spanning \(13.024\)--\(20.142\) s, of which the platform self-gate is \(9.370\pm2.548\) s and the reportability gate that decides KEEP or ABSTAIN is \(0.001\) s, under \(0.01\%\) of the build. An earlier profile timed the complete build under the name of the self-gate stage alongside three of that build's own internal stages, so its six durations overlapped and their sum was not the duration of any pass; it is superseded here. The repeated batch was measured on an Intel Core Ultra 9 275HX (24 logical CPUs, 62 GiB RAM) with Python 3.13; the full-\(n\) passes in the lower block of Table~\ref{tab:timing} are an earlier single-machine run whose hardware was not recorded alongside it. no accelerator is used anywhere in the protocol, and the revision reports mean\(\pm\)SD rather than millisecond precision. The repeated end-to-end value is a reproducibility estimate for this hardware and input size, not a universal performance guarantee. Repeating the whole five-run batch on this machine moved the build mean several-fold depending on background load, by far more than the within-batch standard deviation reported here, so the seconds bound one machine in one state rather than fixing a constant; the batch reported is the one taken with no other work running. The share column of Table~\ref{tab:timing}, not the seconds column, is the quantity that transfers between machines. It nevertheless separates the cost of the fixed composition fit from the new reportability operation, showing that selective reporting adds little computational overhead to the locked workflow.

\paragraph{Sensitivity analyses.} The frozen cutoff remains \(c^\star=0.80\) and is not re-tuned. We report designated-pair threshold sensitivity separately from the complete eligible-neighbor scan, define the eligible set algorithmically, and retain the historical operating pair while treating the complete maximum as a descriptive identifiability stress test. The bounded 400-spot bootstrap is reported alongside full-sample estimates where available.
