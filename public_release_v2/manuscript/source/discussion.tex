Two locked operators turn a training-free composition estimator into one that recovers named tissue objects and states when its malignant coordinate is reportable. In a repeated warm benchmark, a 400-spot openST build took \(22.565\pm2.861\) s across five CPU runs (CV \(12.68\%\)); the in-memory reportability operation itself took \(0.455\) s for 1,000 applications. On four-patient CosMx basal-cell carcinoma the operators recover those objects \citep{Andreatta2024zenodo,Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint}. Ulcerated-nodular Patient B is tumor-rich relative to the more stromal Patient A. The wound time course on Patients C and D is a drop in tumor fraction with a rise in fibroblast and MoMacDC, recovered on D-from-C mixed spots (Baseline tumor \(0.8425\) to Wound \(0.4665\); MoMacDC \(0.0070\) to \(0.1280\)) and in the same direction on typed Patient D cells \citep{Yerly2025preprint,Dvorak1986}.

The protocol is the computational contract: a KEEP/ABSTAIN gate at frozen \(c^\star=0.80\) and a Patient\_ID split that builds mixed spots and reference from disjoint patients \citep{Kiselev2018,deKanter2019,Cable2022}. Mixed-spot construction on four-patient CosMx BCC supplies the tissue object \citep{Stahl2016,Miller2022}, and cell-level geography makes the ulcerated-nodular and wound axes readable before any transfer \citep{Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint}. Spatial fields then name those axes: Patient A is more stromal and Patient B is tumor-rich, and tumor occupancy falls along the wound axis while fibroblast and MoMacDC rise \citep{Dvorak1986,Yerly2025preprint}. The protocol writes a CosMx tumor column beside locked tumor truth on 5686 full-\(n\) KEEP spots (median \(0.6739\)). One frozen cosine holds a KEEP window that survives interpolation on CosMx at \(t=0,0.1,0.2\) and closes only when the malignant column is blended into its neighbor \citep{He2022,Kiselev2018}.

Donor transfer recovers mixed-spot truth across patients \citep{Cable2022,Kleshchevnikov2022}. Overall RMSE on the computed edges is \(0.0659\)--\(0.1349\), type-level Pearson correlation is \(0.7034\)--\(0.7708\), and spot-level Pearson correlation reaches \(0.9528\) on B from A. D from C carries the wound axis \citep{Yerly2025preprint}, and MoMacDC occupancy separates the computed pairs in the same direction. Each edge is scored three ways---whole simplex, malignant column alone, and pseudobulk agreement---so a tight overall RMSE cannot hide a loose tumor column. The evaluation board collects the \(t=0\) interval, the full-\(n\) confirmation, donor RMSE, and the timed openST pass \citep{Schott2024}, and the fifty-cell reference floor sits under mean truth fraction for each of eight shared types.

The same frozen cosine reports KEEP on independent CosMx NSCLC (tumor versus fibroblast \(0.6482\); overall RMSE \(0.1066\), spot-level PCC \(0.8415\)), CosMx colorectal carcinoma (\(0.5058\)), and CosMx HCC (\(0.6486\)) \citep{He2022,Crowell2025preprint,Sun2025spatial}. NSCLC adds a second Patient\_ID transfer edge on a seventeen-type simplex, and colorectal and hepatocellular KEEP calls are gate recurrences on independent CosMx carcinomas. CosMx PDAC at \(0.8616\) is the ABSTAIN face of the same frozen cutoff on that same assay \citep{Pei2025}, which is consistent with a collinearity explanation but does not establish platform independence because the library-level sample is small and pair identity is confounded with platform. The revision therefore reports continuous cosine values and marks Xenium FLEX as paired with Xenium. Native calls remain KEEP on CosMx BCC (cosine \(0.6037\)) and on the three independent KEEP carcinomas, and remain ABSTAIN on CosMx PDAC, HNSCC openST (\(0.9727\)), breast Xenium (\(0.9802\)), and the orthogonal Xenium FLEX library (\(0.9875\)) \citep{Schott2024,Janesick2023,Pei2025}. A tumor coordinate is therefore a KEEP/ABSTAIN decision, and the remaining types continue on the simplex in either case \citep{Kiselev2018,deKanter2019,Danaher2022}.

Spatial profiling reads cellular composition in place, and the mixed-spot readers built for it differ in how they regularize that composition rather than in whether they report one~\citep{Elhanani2023,Rao2021,Moses2022}. Reference-based estimators fit the spot from a profile matrix: RCTD under a Poisson model with an explicit platform effect~\citep{Cable2022}, cell2location under a hierarchical Bayesian model~\citep{Kleshchevnikov2022}, stereoscope under a negative-binomial likelihood~\citep{Andersson2020}, SPOTlight by seeded NMF regression~\citep{ElosuaBayes2021}, and SpatialDWLS by dampened weighted least squares~\citep{Dong2021}. Others change what the fit is conditioned on: DestVI resolves within-type continua rather than a single profile per type~\citep{Lopez2022}, CARD borrows composition between neighboring spots through a spatial prior~\citep{Ma2022}, Tangram aligns cells to positions instead of solving the simplex~\citep{Biancalani2021}, SpatialDecon carries a platform's own background model into the regression~\citep{Danaher2022}, and STdeconvolve drops the reference for a topic model~\citep{Miller2022}. Independent benchmarks place RCTD and cell2location among the leading estimators on this task~\citep{Li2022integrate,Sangaram2024}. What none of them exposes in the matched saved outputs is the same explicit missing-coordinate reportability state evaluated here. The matched audit found method-specific uncertainty fields rather than an equivalent diagnostic, so the claim is limited to this operational reportability state. The matched saved-output audit therefore asks a narrower operational question: all compared outputs contain a malignant coordinate, but none exposes the same explicit missing-coordinate reportability state when the malignant reference is not separable from an eligible neighbor. The quantitative RMSE comparison is useful context, but it is not the contribution and is not interpreted as a universal ranking: a lower RMSE and a narrower posterior interval answer different questions from whether a coordinate should be written at all. The reportability operator is compatible with such estimators because it adds a decision layer around a composition output rather than replacing the underlying fit. This comparison is limited to the matched saved outputs and is not a claim that every version of every estimator has been exhaustively re-run.

The reportability operator is a new mixed-spot contribution, even though refusal has precedents in single-cell annotation. scmap leaves a cell unassigned below a similarity cutoff, and CHETAH stops at an unassigned or intermediate node when confidence is insufficient~\citep{Kiselev2018,deKanter2019}; both withhold a label for one cell. Moving that refusal onto the mixed-spot simplex is what lets a per-spot composition carry a malignant coordinate only where the reference can support one, and it is deliberately partial: the remaining types are still reported, so an ABSTAIN spot is a composition with one coordinate missing rather than a discarded spot. Because the cutoff is frozen from the fixed baseline implementation~\citep{Fu2026spgd} rather than fitted per substrate, the eight KEEP and ABSTAIN calls above are out-of-sample applications of one rule, and CosMx PDAC abstaining on the platform that carries all four KEEP calls is consistent with a collinearity explanation, while the present design does not establish platform independence~\citep{Pei2025,He2022,Crowell2025preprint,Sun2025spatial}.

Three boundaries delimit the claim: the wound axis is a CosMx BCC object on Patients C and D, so the independent NSCLC, colorectal, and hepatocellular results are gate and transfer recurrences rather than a second wound time course~\citep{Yerly2022,Yerly2025preprint,Dvorak1986,He2022,Crowell2025preprint,Sun2025spatial}; the C-from-D row is recorded rather than recomputed here and is what closes the four-edge donor matrix; and the cutoff is frozen from the fixed baseline implementation, so this work reports where that rule lands rather than an optimum. Clinical outcome analysis lies outside this Methods object.

\paragraph{Revision audit and limitations.} The revision separates the historical designated-pair estimand from a descriptive complete eligible-neighbor scan. Across the 51 complete eligible rows, the candidate maximum cosine had mean \(0.674\) (10,000 library-cluster bootstrap 95\% CI \(0.579\)--\(0.781\)), median \(0.725\) (CI \(0.678\)--\(0.814\)), and 29.4\% of rows at or above \(0.80\) (CI \(6.3\)--\(68.4\%\)); this interval is an audit result, not a locked replacement operating rule. The ABSTAIN renormalization check retained simplex closure to \(1.1\times10^{-15}\), with conditional-neighbor RMSE \(0.1761\)--\(0.1890\) and bias \(0.0249\)--\(0.0507\) in the available test edges. A floor-25 audit retained 9--10 shared types versus 8 at floors 50--100. Twelve bounded downstream fits across three directed edges and floors 25, 50, 75, and 100 showed edge-dependent changes from floor 25 to 50 and identical estimates at floors 50--100 within edge because retained support was unchanged; this is a bounded sensitivity result, not a universal floor-invariance claim. The matched saved-output comparator gave RMSE \(0.1213\) for RCTD, \(0.1542\) for SPGD, and \(0.2487\) for cell2location; its uncertainty fields are method-specific rather than an equivalent malignant-coordinate gate. The locked primary truth remains a cell-count share, not transcript mass. The saved truth does not contain a cell-to-spot transcript-total mapping, so an RNA-weighted sensitivity is not claimed.
