# Discussion rewrite rationale (2026-09-12)

The V3 Discussion was rewritten as a conventional scientific discussion rather than a revision log. The text now uses four substantive paragraphs: reference separability/reportability, donor-disjoint transfer, relation to existing estimators, and scope/limitations. It retains verified quantitative results from the current Results and Methods files, including the full-(n) CosMx KEEP analysis, dose-response, complete eligible-neighbor scan, donor metrics, abstention-bias audit, floor sensitivity, comparator audit, and repeated timing.

The rewrite removes internal workflow language (for example, “revision audit”, “candidate corrected rule”, and “author-locked”) and avoids implying that the software archive is the scientific contribution. It describes the implementation as a standalone Methods protocol with fixed-baseline provenance and places public-resource and licensing boundaries in the limitations/data-availability context. Claims about platform independence are explicitly limited to evidence consistent with collinearity; the text does not infer a causal assay effect from the small, confounded library set.

The remaining author decisions are substantive rather than editorial: whether to lock one uniform malignant-neighbor selection rule for a future analysis, whether the NanoString agreement permits redistribution of derived reference material, and how to allocate the expanded figures under the journal’s display-object limit. No new results were invented in this rewrite.
